## 🌟 What is STRATRONIX?

STRATRONIX is the creator of **PAA (Personal AI Office Assistant)** — a new product category that combines dedicated AI hardware with instant messaging integration.

Our flagship product, **STA-100 Workie**, is a compact AI server that runs 24/7, connecting directly to your existing messaging apps (WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Teams, Feishu, Discord, WeChat) — **no app download needed**.

## 💬 How It Works

```
You (WhatsApp/Telegram/Slack/Teams/Feishu)
    ↓ message
STA-100 Workie (on your desk, 24/7)
    ↓ local AI processing
OpenClaw Agent
    ↓ API calls to your LLM
Response back to your chat
```

**All data stays on your device** — GDPR compliant, data sovereignty guaranteed.
